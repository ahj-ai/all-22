The Hard Count

The State of the League

Draft Week · Monday, September 7, 2026

Gentlemen. Ten of you are back, two of you are not, and two of you have no idea what you have signed up for. The board resets tomorrow at three.

Four years of tape says one thing louder than anything else: the number one seed has never won this league. Not once. Four for four. Second, fourth, fourth, second. You can spend fourteen weeks proving you are the best team here, and all it buys you is a better seat to watch somebody else lift it.

Ask Pass me the Bijan — except he solved it. In 2024 that roster put up 1,751, still the most anyone has ever scored here, went 12-2 as the one seed, and finished fourth. So last year he stopped being clever: $73 on Bijan Robinson, the most expensive man in league history, $161 of a $200 budget on three players, named the franchise after one of them, and won it from the two seed. Subtlety is dead and he shot it himself.

Now hold that next to The Ja'Marjacksons, who ran the identical play — $68 on Ja'Marr Chase, named the team after him — and went 5-9 with the worst all-play mark in the league, a .299. Same conviction, same budget, opposite ending. That is the entire game in two rosters, and it is why tomorrow afternoon matters more than any Sunday will.

Worth remembering before you talk yourself into a price: 42 of last year's 168 players went for a single dollar. A quarter of every roster in this league was effectively free.

The Ja'Marjacksons and System QB are out. System QB leaves with the annoying distinction of never once posting a losing record here, which is more than the rest of us can say. Two new managers take their seats, with no history, no receipts, and no idea. Welcome to it.

And the rest of the tape is unchanged. Take this Tunsilitis made six roster moves all season, went 4-10, and posted the unluckiest year in four seasons — those scores deserved six wins. Milk Hunter went the other way with 78 moves, more than the bottom four teams combined, and landed on exactly 7-7. Milked that wire raw for four months to finish .500.

As for me: four seasons, unlucky in every single one, a running tab of three full wins the schedule owes me, and the third-best all-play in the building last year for a fourth-place finish. I also spent $192 of $200 and bought zero dollar players, which means I left eight bucks on the table and went home anyway. Egbukake, truly.

Auction. $200. Monday 3pm. Everyone's 0-0. Bring bad intentions.
